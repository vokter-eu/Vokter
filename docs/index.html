FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

Is it really free?
Yes — genuinely. Vokter is open source and runs on your own computer, so there's no server for us to pay for and nothing to meter. No account, no subscription, no per-token bill.
Does my data leave my computer?
No. Everything runs locally — there's no telemetry and nothing is synced to a cloud. Your files and memory are encrypted on disk, and the key lives in your OS keyring.
What can it do today, and what's coming later?
Today it's a local assistant: chat that remembers you, chat with your documents (with citations), reminders, and voice — all offline. Acting on the web, bills, delegation and mobile are on the roadmap, clearly marked above under “What it does today” and “On the roadmap”.
What hardware do I need?
Everyday laptops are fine. Vokter automatically picks a model sized to your machine — a light one on modest hardware, a more capable one if you have a stronger CPU or GPU. More power means faster, sharper answers, but you don't need a gaming rig to start.
Is it really open source?
Yes — AGPL-3.0. You can read every line, verify what it does, and build it yourself. View the source on GitHub.
